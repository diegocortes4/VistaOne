SMS / Text Message Opt-In Consent

This page describes how VistaOne Properties LLC collects SMS consent from contacts.

How Contacts Opt In

Contacts provide explicit SMS consent by completing the property evaluation form at https://www.vistaoneproperties.com/#evaluate. The form contains a clearly visible, unchecked checkbox labeled with the full disclosure language shown below. Submission of the form without checking this box does not constitute SMS consent.

📱 SMS Consent Checkbox (as shown on the website form)

By submitting this form, you agree to receive SMS/text messages from VistaOne Properties LLC at the number you provided. We send information about land and real estate opportunities, follow-ups, and service updates. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Text HELP for help or email info@vistaoneproperties.com. See our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Key Disclosures

  • Opt-in is voluntary. The checkbox is unchecked by default and is not required to submit the form.
  • SMS opt-in data and phone numbers are never shared, sold, or disclosed to third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Contacts may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on interaction.
  • Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • This service is intended for individuals 18 years of age or older.

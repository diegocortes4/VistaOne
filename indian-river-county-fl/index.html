Parcels are typically a quarter acre or more. Flood zone and wetland status are the deciding factors, along with whether the lot sits on an interior street rather than a through road.

Why Indian River County has so many vacant lots

Indian River County sits on the Treasure Coast around Vero Beach. Vero Lake Estates, a rural residential subdivision inland from the city, was platted decades ago and holds a substantial stock of vacant lots. Like the other inland subdivisions along this stretch of coast, it was sold on affordability and proximity to the beach, and a large share of the lots were bought by people who never moved down.

What drives value in Indian River County

Vero Lake Estates parcels are typically a quarter acre or more. Flood zone and wetland status are the deciding factors here, and they matter more than in the drier inland counties because of the surrounding low ground. Interior street position is the other consideration. Vero Beach itself has grown steadily, which supports demand for buildable lots in the inland subdivisions within reach of it.

Who sells us land in Indian River County

Vero Lake Estates owners typically bought an affordable inland parcel with the beach in mind and never acted on it. Because lots here were inexpensive relative to coastal parcels, multiple-lot holdings are common. Inherited parcels make up a large share of what we see, often held by heirs several states away who have no connection to the area beyond an annual tax bill.

What we check on a Indian River County parcel

  • Wetlands. The most important one. Most builders will not take on wetland permitting, so a wetland parcel is usually not something we can buy.
  • Flood zone and wetlands
  • Lot size, a quarter acre or larger
  • Interior neighbourhood position
  • Road access

Flood zone and wetland extent are the priority checks in Vero Lake Estates. We pull the county GIS layers and cross-reference FEMA mapping, since the inland ground here can be wetter than the plat suggests. Lot size, road access and interior position complete the research.

Indian River County questions

Is Vero Lake Estates near the beach?

It sits inland from Vero Beach rather than on the coast, which is why the lots were affordable when they were sold and why so many were bought speculatively. Vero Beach itself has grown steadily, which supports demand for buildable inland parcels.

How wet is the ground in Vero Lake Estates?

It varies considerably parcel to parcel, and the plat will not tell you. We check both the county wetland layer and FEMA flood mapping before quoting, because the inland ground here can be wetter than it appears.

Do I need to live in Florida to sell?

No. Most owners we work with live out of state. Closing documents are handled remotely with a Florida title company and a mobile or online notary.

What if I owe back taxes or association dues?

Common, and not a barrier. Balances are normally settled at closing out of the sale proceeds. Tell us the situation so the offer reflects it accurately.

Do I need a survey or appraisal?

No. We research the parcel ourselves from public records. You are never asked to pay for a survey, appraisal or inspection.

What does it cost me?

No agent commission and no listing fee. Closing costs are handled as set out in the purchase contract.
